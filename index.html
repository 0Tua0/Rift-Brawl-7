Room codeBoth players pick a fighter in the P1 slot. One player types a room code and clicks Host. The other types the same code and clicks Join. The host picks the map and presses Start online match. Each player uses the P1 keys on their own device.

P1: A/D move · W jump (double jump) · S fast-fall / drop through · F attack · G special
P2: ← → move · ↑ jump · ↓ fast-fall · . attack · / special
3 normal attacks: Attack = forward, Up+Attack = launcher, Down+Attack = low sweep. Special = signature move with a visible cooldown bar (under your feet and on your HUD card). Up+Special is a short-cooldown recovery leap (once per airtime).
Combos: land a hit, then press Attack again (any direction) to chain up to 4 hits. The 4th is a launching FINISHER. You can also cancel a hit into your Special. Hits landed in quick succession build a combo meter that adds bonus damage. Esc or P pauses.
Defense: hold Shield (P1: H, P2: comma) to block. Shield + a direction rolls or air-dodges. Shield + Attack grabs (beats shields). While holding: Attack pummels, A/D throws forward or back, W throws up, S slams the foe into the ground and they bounce. A grabbed player can mash buttons to escape. Parry: tap Shield just before a hit lands to cancel it, stagger the attacker and gain meter (spamming it has a short cooldown). Final Smash: hits fill the gold meter under your HUD card; at 100% press the Final Smash key (P1: T, P2: semicolon) for a screen-wide blast. Items drop in: heart heals 25%, star adds meter, bomb is a trap. Hazards: Skyline has lightning storms, Twin Peaks has lava geysers. Modes: Team Battle (P1+P3 vs P2+P4) and Training. Boss Fight: go solo or team up against the Titan in its own arena. It has three phases with warned attacks and gets angrier below a third of its health. Landing the last hit plays a finishing-move cutscene.
Extras: every fighter has a unique-named Final Smash (T / semicolon). Taunt: R (P1) or quote (P2). You grab ledges automatically when you fall near a main platform edge; press Jump to climb up or Down to drop. Duplicate characters get a recolor. The camera zooms in when fighters get close, and the last KO plays in slow motion. Gamepads: pad 1 = P1, pad 2 = P2 (stick or D-pad move, A jump, X attack, B special, Y Final Smash, LB taunt, RB or RT shield).
